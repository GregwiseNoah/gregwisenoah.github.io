Carving Cavities in the Cosmos

Simulations of AGN jets in galaxy groups and clusters

Master's thesis at the Leibniz Institute for Astrophysics Potsdam, supervised by Dr. Rainer Weinberger

The supermassive black holes at the centres of galaxy groups and clusters launch jets that push aside the surrounding hot gas, carving the low-density cavities seen in X-ray observations. These jets are thought to heat the gas and keep it from cooling onto the central galaxy.

I run hydrodynamic simulations of these jets with the moving-mesh code Arepo, using the jet model described in Weinberger et al. (2023), and focus primarily on galaxy groups. I checked the idealised initial conditions against halos from the IllustrisTNG simulations.
