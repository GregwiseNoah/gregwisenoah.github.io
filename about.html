About

I'm Ashwin, a master's student in astrophysics at the University of Potsdam. I work in computational astrophysics, using hydrodynamical simulations to understand how gas behaves in and around galaxies.

Currently, I am wrapping up my Master's degree and applying for PhDs starting in 2027.

Alongside my studies, I work as a student research assistant at GFZ Potsdam on aurora and magnetometer data.

Away from the screen, I like being outdoors in nature and cooking, and I can spend far too long choosing the colours for a plot.
